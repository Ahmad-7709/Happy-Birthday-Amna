From The Depths of My Heart

A Letter For You

To My Dearest Zibaam, 17 August • Forever & Always

Happy Birthday to the most loyal, loving, caring, kind-hearted, and incredible woman I have ever known.

Looking at you today, radiant and full of grace, my heart overflows with gratitude I can never fully repay. Exactly one year ago, through the sacred bond of our Nikah, Almighty Allah answered my deepest prayers and blessed me with a piece of His paradise on earth. Walking into this life as your husband has been the greatest honor of my existence, my supervisor.

If you were to ask me what I cherish most about our first year of marriage, I could never point to just one thing. It is the warmth of your laugh echoing across a crowded room. It is how fiercely and beautifully you care for our little world and the people around you. It is the way you silently hold my hand when the world feels heavy, instantly reminding me that we can weather absolutely anything as long as we are side by side.

Every single milestone we have conquered together, from walking the historic streets of Moscow holding hands, to the profound pride of standing on the stage of HSE University graduating by your side, has been etched into my soul. Yet, as majestic as Russia was, my heart finds its truest sanctuary in our quiet, everyday poetry. I cherish our late-night talks, our chaotic midnight runs for popcorn, the sweet aroma of halwa we made together, and the sound of your laughter as we tried to learn dance steps in our living room. You turn the most ordinary evenings into sacred memories, and you make dreaming about our future feel like the safest place to be.

Amna, you have turned my life into art. One year of being your husband has shown me that every year with you will only grow richer, deeper, and more meaningful than the last. I look at you today, and I fall in love with you all over again, even more intensely than the day of our Nikah.

My wish for you on this beautiful birthday is that you feel as deeply appreciated, cherished, and celebrated as you truly are. May your heart be full of a peace that never fades, your days brimming with endless delight, and your wildest dreams unfolding one by one, with me right here beside you, fiercely cheering you on for the rest of our days.

Thank you for choosing me, for loving me, and for being my home every single day of this past year.

Happy Birthday, My Brilliant Graduate, My Beautiful Wife, My Shonaa Bubu.

With all my love, forever and always, Tmhara Motkaa Duduu Fuduu

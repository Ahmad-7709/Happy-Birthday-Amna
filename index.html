A Letter For You

To My Dearest Wife, On Your Special Day

Happy Birthday to the most breathtaking, kind-hearted, and incredible woman I have ever known.

If you were to ask me what I cherish most about our life together, I could never point to just one thing. It is the effortless warmth of your laugh across a crowded room. It is how fiercely you care for the people around you. It is the way you hold my hand when the world feels heavy and remind me that we can weather anything as long as we are side by side.

Every single year with you is richer and more meaningful than the last. You make ordinary moments feel like poetry and turn every quiet evening into a sanctuary. I look at you today, radiant and full of grace, and I fall in love with you all over again.

My wish for you this year is that you feel as deeply appreciated, cherished, and celebrated as you truly are. May your heart be full of peace, your days brimming with delight, and your dreams unfolding one by one with me right here cheering you on.

Thank you for choosing me every day. You are my best friend, my soulmate, and my forever home.

Forever & always yours, With All My Love
